## 6. How PromptShield differs (claims to make carefully)

1. **Objective success oracle** (canary in any of 6 encodings + forbidden tool execution) rather than LLM-graded success. *Evidence needed:* show a case where an LLM grader would disagree.
2. **Conformal-gated cascade** — escalation decided by a prediction-set rule with a per-class coverage guarantee. *Evidence needed:* coverage vs. α plots (we produce them), cost/latency vs. judge-only, behaviour under distribution shift (OOD split).
3. **Platform engineered securely** (audit chain, RBAC, etc.) — an engineering contribution, not a research one.

Be explicit about limits: static payload list, small number of target apps, locally hosted 7B-class models, guarantee holds only under exchangeability.
